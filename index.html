Good to Know

Common questions

Does acupuncture hurt?

Acupuncture needles are hair-thin, and most people feel little to nothing on insertion. You may notice a dull ache, warmth or heaviness. Many clients find sessions deeply relaxing.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what we're treating. Acute issues often respond within a few visits, while chronic or training-related problems may benefit from a short series, then occasional maintenance. We'll map it out together.

Can I train after a session?

Usually light activity is fine. Kate will give you specific guidance based on your treatment, and we can schedule sessions around races and key workouts.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing that can be rolled up at the knees and elbows, or athletic wear you can move in.

Do you take insurance?

Please reach out and ask. Details on payment and coverage will be shared when you book.
